"The year is 2025... The Earth's ozone layer was destroyed in the latter part of the twentieth century." Some serious Mad Max vides here. But still, a great game with a theme song that made it pretty easy to turn Heavy Metal!

It's been a while since I posted a video, I have a few songs ready but the problem is always finding time to film and edit.

In this video I didn’t get a chance to mess around with a green screen since it was not easy to find a place to put myself in the game, so instead I thought I'd try Apple's new cinematic mode on the iPhone amongst other video editing tricks. Needless to say I'm not happy with the result, the amount of hours it took to edit this thing and the not so great result, I'm not using this mode again and Apple, please make things more universally compatible instead of only looking good on your own hardware.

Original credits to Mark Cooksey for the awesome composition. An absolutely incredible composition and pretty hardcore synth sounds, very ahead of its time. The Commodore 64 version is very cool too.

And for those that don't know me, I grew up playing Amstrad games with an Amstrad my grandad got us from when I was born. That very same Amstrad CPC6128 is featured in this video! I am very proud to have it featured in my videos as well as using it to sample the original audio snippets I use in these covers.

And finally, a very special thanks to Rula Vam for many of the video ideas, direction, camera work, prop making and cat herding. Thanks for not only supporting my silliness but encouraging it and contributing to it too!
