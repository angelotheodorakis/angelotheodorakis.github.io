This is my first video, the one that started my idea for a full album of Amstrad Video game Metal covers.

I grew up playing Amstrad games. This is one of my favourite video game themes. It just gives me that nostalgic feeling of childhood.

I have had this cover sitting in my projects library since 2007 so I decided to give it a remake and post it. 

Original credits to Thorsten Mitschele for the awesome composition. I can't even begin to imagine how difficult it would be to program something like this back in the early 90's.
