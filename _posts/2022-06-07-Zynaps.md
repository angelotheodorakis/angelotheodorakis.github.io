(Put the disk in the disk reader, type run"zynaps", press enter... promising title screen while the game is loading... wait for it...) BANG!

THE most awesome music intro of an Amstrad game hits you in the face. In my opinion, the most complete composition of any Amstrad game I know of. The song touches prog rock levels, multiple melody changes and harmonies, complex drums, an unbelievable amount of stuff from a machine with just 8bit sound and 3 channels capabilities. 

Inspired by my previous video covering Prehistorik 2, I am going to cover many of my favourite Amstrad game themes so this is the second video of (hopefully) many more to come.

I grew up playing Amstrad games with an Amstrad my grandad got us from when I was born. This song just gives me that nostalgic feeling of childhood. I still  have my Amstrad and after I finally got it working I started featuring it in my latest videos! (and yes, I still play games on it from time to time!)

Original credits to Dave Rogers for the awesome composition. As I said my other video, I can't even begin to imagine how difficult it would be to program something like this back in the late 80's. Thank you sir for defining the sound of my childhood.
