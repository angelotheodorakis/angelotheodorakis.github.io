M.A.S.K. (acronym for "Mobile Armored Strike Kommand") in their battle against V.E.N.O.M. (acronym for "Vicious Evil Network Of Mayhem") is a cartoon from the 80's I grew up watching on Junior TV. Besides obviously greatly enjoying the cartoon as a kid I also played the games on my Amstrad.

Mask III - Venom strikes back was the third game in an Amstrad series of MASK games, a very fun side scroller game, quite tough too. The intro music of course was a great song which I thought deserved a makeover with some heavy 8-string guitars, my own personal solo (hinting at another Amstrad game for those that might recognise) and a bit of a funky twist cause why not.

In this video, besides my usual silliness and some improvements on my editing skills, I am also introducing my new personal Logo, designed by the one and only Rula Vam!

Original credits to Ben Daglish for the awesome music. I watched an interview of his recently and he looked like an awesome dude I would have loved to have met. I'm dedicating this cover to him which I believe he would have enjoyed.

Repeating myself once more, I can't even begin to imagine how difficult it was to compose music using Basic and make it so catchy and interesting too. I hope my rendition does it justice.

And for those that don't know me, I grew up playing Amstrad games with an Amstrad my grandad got us from when I was born. That very same Amstrad CPC6128 is featured in this video! I am very proud to have it featured in my videos as well as using it to sample the original audio snippets I use in these covers.

And finally, a very special thanks to Rula Vam for all her support, love, and incredible graphics design and video direction skills. Once more a huge thank you for not only tolerating my silliness but encouraging it and contributing to it too!
