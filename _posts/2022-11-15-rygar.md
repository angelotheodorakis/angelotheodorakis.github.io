Next video, or should I say... VHS tape, in my series of Amstrad games music metal covers is a game that took me years to rediscover. All I had was a distant memory of how the game might look, from playing it at a very young age. It had been stuck in my head for many years and I reached a borderline obsession to find it. One day, I randomly ran into the game in a top 150 Amstrad games YouTube video and instantly recognised it! I felt an unbelievable amount of relief that even brought tears to my eyes.

Carrying on from my previous videos and sticking to the Amstrad themed music metal covers (for now), this one was so much fun to make, in the music and video style of 80's - 90's glam metal (aka hair metal).

Original credits to a veteran in the industry, known for numerous games he composed for, David Whittaker. A theme that gave the game that extra sense of adventure and definitely an earworm. 

And finally, a very special thanks to Rula Vam for many of the video ideas, direction, camera work and of course, graphics design. Thanks for not only supporting my silliness but encouraging it and contributing to it too!
