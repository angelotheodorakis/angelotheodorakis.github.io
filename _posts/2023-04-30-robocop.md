"Serve the public trust" - I'm here to serve in the form of a new theme cover. 

One of my favourite movies as a kid and of course, an addictive Amstrad video game, with what was groundbreaking at the time, a voiceover of Robocop's directives.

Another Amstrad themed music metal cover, with a Djent twist (plus some famous song references for those who will recognise them). I very much enjoyed recording and mixing this one, it was tough to make it sound heavy and djenty, but I really like the result.

Original credits to Jonathan Dunn for the awesome composition. A theme that gave the game a memorable soundtrack, definitely an earworm. 

I grew up playing Amstrad games with an Amstrad my grandad got us from when I was born. That very same Amstrad CPC6128 is featured in this video!
